PROJECT BRIEF

You are a Business Intelligence Analyst at a fictional European e-commerce company.
Management wants a decision-support system that explains current performance, identifies
growth and profitability drivers, evaluates marketing efficiency and highlights areas requiring action.

Required outputs:
1. Reproducible Python ETL pipeline.
2. PostgreSQL database and documented schema.
3. SQL KPI/analysis queries.
4. Power BI executive dashboard.
5. Customer/product/marketing drill-down pages.
6. Statistical analysis of at least one business hypothesis.
7. Revenue or demand forecast with an explicit baseline and error metric.
8. Excel scenario model for one management decision.
9. Executive summary with 3-5 evidence-based recommendations.

Suggested KPIs:
Revenue, gross profit, gross margin %, orders, units, AOV, customers, repeat customer rate,
return rate, refund value, discount %, CAC proxy, ROAS, conversion rate and revenue growth.
